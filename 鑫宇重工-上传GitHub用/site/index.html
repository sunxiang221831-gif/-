Company profile

Quality is in place.

Yantai Xinyu Heavy Industry Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer of construction machinery and a specialised producer of small engineering tire loaders. We develop, research and build trackless equipment for underground work.

The plant combines advanced processing equipment, strong technical capability and modern management — supported by a comprehensive hydraulic pump and motor test bench, an independent enterprise technology centre, complete production capacity and a fast after-sales service system.

“Technology innovation as the driving force, market demand as the goal, user experience at the centre — integrity in management, excellence as the purpose, and value for our customers as the mission.” Han Chunyu · General Manager
  • Hydraulic test bench Comprehensive pump and motor test rig for every unit before dispatch.
  • Technology centre Independent R&D for trackless underground equipment.
  • Full production capacity Complete manufacturing chain, from fabrication to final assembly.
  • After-sales system Convenient and fast service, wherever the machine is working.

Product range

Five series. One standard.

Every machine is configured for the job it will actually do — engine, axle, bucket and canopy chosen to suit the tunnel, the ore and the shift pattern.

Mining Scraper Series

Trackless loaders built for narrow tunnels, low headings and hard-rock headings — the LZ30E tire loader and the 928 mine loader.

  • Variable-displacement pumps and motors originating from Sauer, USA
  • Heavy-duty front and rear drive axles — greater load capacity, markedly lower failure rate
  • Pilot control with fully hydraulic steering — easy, flexible operation
  • Centralised lubrication system fitted as standard across the machine
  • Flexible cable winding device with automatic protection for over-released cables
  • Compact, articulated chassis with a central hinge — low failure rate, high comfort

OptionalVisual-distance remote control / remote video control · automatic fire-extinguishing system

  • 2,000–3,500 kg rated load
  • 0.8–2.0 m³ bucket
  • 25–30° gradeability
  • 75–90 kN traction

Mining Loader Series

Purpose-built for underground metal mines — iron ore, gold ore, copper ore, lead-zinc ore and aluminium ore. Models 929, ZL946 and ZL932.

  • Italian Manulli pipework — double-layer rubber, double-layer steel wire, H-shaped double-sealed joints, 42CrMo pin shafts
  • Q460 high-density frame: strong load bearing, resistant to torsion, deformation and tension
  • VHP ultra-high-pressure oil pump with single-pump diversion — saves energy, cuts consumption
  • Rock-specific bucket as standard, wear-resistant alloy-cast main blade and alloy shovel teeth
  • Height-adjustable roof shed — only 2.4 m from ground to canopy
  • Mining-grade nylon 20-level anti-cut tyres fitted as standard
  • Standard secondary water filtration and purification; optional imported three-way purifier
  • 2,000 kg rated load
  • 0.8 m³ bucket
  • 16 MPa hydraulic system
  • 2.4 m canopy height

Loader Series

The foot-tiger loader works agilely in low, tight spaces — a dependable helper for owners who need to move material fast.

  • High lifting force with automatic unloading and levelling — shorter work-device cycle
  • Caliper disc brake with high braking efficiency
  • Split-type hydraulic torque converter — not prone to high temperatures, easy to maintain
  • External automatic inflation system — inflate anytime, anywhere
  • Horizontal-bar work lever for comfortable, convenient operation

Suitable forBasements · farms · greenhouses · tunnels

  • Low-headroom design
  • Automatic levelling
  • Disc brake
  • On-board inflation

Transportation Mixer Series

Truck-mounted and self-loading mixers that mix on site and pour where the work is. Available in 3.5 m³, 5 m³ and 6 m³.

  • Five-speed plus one reverse, fully synchronised transmission
  • 330 transfer case with two-wheel / four-wheel drive switching
  • Front hydraulic steering with air brake; heavy-duty rear air brake
  • Hydraulic mixing with automatic water addition
  • Water tank mounted on the rear side; 1.2 cm impact-resistant mixing-tank plate
  • 140 heavy-duty plated transmission shaft
  • Hydraulic components covered by a 12-month warranty
  • 3.5 / 5 / 6 m³
  • 3–4 min mixing
  • 1,000 mm discharge height
  • 4WD switchable

Rubber Hose for Mining

Cord-wound, fibre-wound and specialist hose for the toughest duty — plus conveyor belts, high-pressure oxygen pipe and steel wire suction hose.

  • Cord winding, fibre winding and sandblasting hose
  • Cord suction and steel wire suction hose
  • Oil-resistant, acid & alkali resistant and high-temperature resistant hose
  • Conveyor belts, high-pressure oxygen pipe, steel braided pipe, hydraulic hose, steam hose
  • Research and development of new products undertaken to specification
